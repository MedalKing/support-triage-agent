# Transport damage

Applies when goods arrived damaged.

1. Check the delivery event note for a damage remark on the proof of delivery (POD). A remark on the POD means the carrier accepted the damage.
2. With a POD remark: propose `reship_order` for the damaged quantity (needs approval) and `contact_carrier` to file a claim.
3. Without a POD remark: ask the customer for photos within 7 days before proposing a reship.
